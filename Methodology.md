# Methodology: Target Company Research (Pune Cluster)

## 1. Search Strategy & Hub Identification
Instead of broad web scraping, I targeted high-density "innovation nodes" in Pune:
- **Venture Center (NCL Innovation Park)**: I manually audited the startup portfolios to find R&D-heavy MSMEs.
- **Industrial Zones**: Focused on Bhosari and Chakan clusters, specifically looking for firms with "Manufacturing" and "R&D" listed in their LinkedIn/Google profiles.

## 2. Research & Verification Process
I used a "Triangulation" method to ensure data accuracy:
- **Primary Source**: Company websites and product catalogs to verify C1 (Manufacturer status) and C3 (Differentiators).
- **Deep Research AI**: Used Gemini’s deep-thinking mode to verify specific location data and operational focus, filtering out pure traders/distributors.
- **Regulatory/Financial Check**: Verified revenue estimates via Tofler/Zauba Corp and looked for DSIR-recognized R&D units to score C4 (Technical DM).

## 3. The "Federer" Filter
I applied a strict rejection logic:
- **Auto-Fail**: Any firm that appeared to be a CRO (Service-based) or a Generic Pharma/Trader was immediately disqualified.
- **Yield**: I investigated ~75 companies to find the 25 that met the criteria (Manufacturer, India-based, Differentiated, Technical DM, Growing Sector, and Growth Signals).
