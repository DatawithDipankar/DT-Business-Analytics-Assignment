# DeepThought Business Analytics: Target Company Research (Pune Cluster)

## Overview
[cite_start]This repository contains the simulation assignment for the **Business Analytics Internship** at DeepThought[cite: 1]. [cite_start]The objective was to identify and analyze "Federer" companies—high-growth, promoter-driven manufacturing MSMEs—within the specialty biotechnology and diagnostics sectors[cite: 6, 8, 11].

## Research Scope
- [cite_start]**City Hub**: Pune, Maharashtra (Clusters: Bhosari, Chakan, Hinjawadi)[cite: 31, 245, 248].
- [cite_start]**Segments**: Specialty Biotech & Specialty Diagnostics/Life-Science Tools[cite: 33].
- **Target Profile**: Manufacturing MSMEs with Rs. 50Cr–Rs. [cite_start]500Cr revenue and technical leadership[cite: 6, 40].

## Repository Structure
- [cite_start]**`Assignment_Target_Company_Research.csv`**: Contains detailed profiles of 25 qualified target companies and 4 documented "Fail" cases[cite: 108, 117]. [cite_start]Each entry includes per-criterion evidence (C1-C6), Federer Scores, and personalized outreach hooks[cite: 76, 116].
- [cite_start]**`Methodology.md`**: Outlines the manual research process used to identify innovation nodes (e.g., Venture Center) and the verification steps taken to avoid AI hallucination[cite: 118, 119, 251].
- [cite_start]**`Scale_Up_Proposal.md`**: A strategic proposal for building a list of 1,000 ICP-qualified companies in 30 days using automated sourcing, AI-driven scoring, and human-in-the-loop verification[cite: 123, 130].

## The Federer Scoring Framework
[cite_start]Each company was evaluated against six weighted criteria to ensure a strong fit with DeepThought’s "Scientific Execution" model[cite: 35, 40, 59]:
1. [cite_start]**Manufacturer (C1)**: Physical product production[cite: 42].
2. [cite_start]**India-based (C2)**: Local HQ or manufacturing presence[cite: 44].
3. [cite_start]**Differentiated (C3)**: Patents, R&D depth, or niche technical products[cite: 45].
4. [cite_start]**Technical Decision-Maker (C4)**: Founders/MDs with PhDs or engineering backgrounds[cite: 46].
5. [cite_start]**Growing Sector (C5)**: Operating in areas with policy or market tailwinds[cite: 48].
6. [cite_start]**Active Growth Signals (C6)**: Hiring, facility expansion, or new certifications[cite: 50, 51].
