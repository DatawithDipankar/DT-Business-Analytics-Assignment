# DeepThought Business Analytics: Target Company Research (Pune Cluster)

## Overview
This repository contains the simulation assignment for the **Business Analytics Internship** at DeepThought. The objective was to identify and analyze "Federer" companies high-growth, promoter-driven manufacturing MSMEs—within the specialty biotechnology and diagnostics sectors.

## Research Scope
- **City Hub**: Pune, Maharashtra (Clusters: Bhosari, Chakan, Hinjawadi).
- **Segments**: Specialty Biotech & Specialty Diagnostics/Life-Science Tools.
- **Target Profile**: Manufacturing MSMEs with Rs. 50Cr–Rs. 500Cr revenue and technical leadership.

## Repository Structure
- **`Assignment_Target_Company_Research.csv`**: Contains detailed profiles of 25 qualified target companies and 4 documented "Fail" cases. Each entry includes per-criterion evidence (C1-C6), Federer Scores, and personalized outreach hooks.
- **`Methodology.md`**: Outlines the manual research process used to identify innovation nodes (e.g., Venture Center) and the verification steps taken to avoid AI hallucination.
- **`Scale_Up_Proposal.md`**: A strategic proposal for building a list of 1,000 ICP-qualified companies in 30 days using automated sourcing, AI-driven scoring, and human-in-the-loop verification.

## The Federer Scoring Framework
Each company was evaluated against six weighted criteria to ensure a strong fit with DeepThought’s "Scientific Execution" model:
1. **Manufacturer (C1)**: Physical product production.
2. **India-based (C2)**: Local HQ or manufacturing presence.
3. **Differentiated (C3)**: Patents, R&D depth, or niche technical products.
4. **Technical Decision-Maker (C4)**: Founders/MDs with PhDs or engineering backgrounds.
5. **Growing Sector (C5)**: Operating in areas with policy or market tailwinds.
6. **Active Growth Signals (C6)**: Hiring, facility expansion, or new certifications.
